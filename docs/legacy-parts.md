# Preserved legacy parts

The v1.0.1 34 mm preferred edge-registration stand, 32 mm narrow stand, original cradle and original lift-off hood retain their exact CAD/STL bytes. Their original G-code and previews are also unchanged. Previous tags and DOI archives remain historical references.

| Legacy asset | CAD | STL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 34 mm stand | [source](../cad/fixed_ir_source_34mm.scad), `part="source_stand"` | [mesh](../stl/source_stand_34mm.stl) |
| 32 mm stand | [source](../cad/fixed_ir_source_32mm.scad), `part="source_stand"` | [mesh](../stl/source_stand_32mm.stl) |
| Original cradle | Either source, `part="detector_cradle"` | [mesh](../stl/detector_cradle.stl) |
| Lift-off hood | [source](../cad/ambient_cover.scad), `mode="print"` | [roof-down mesh](../stl/ambient_cover_110mm_roof_down.stl) |

Other legacy selectors are illustrative hardware or sections. The original hood is retained without redesign; it is incompatible with the extended rig. The 32 mm stand has no supplied G-code. Historical G-code filenames identify Ender-3 V3 KE profiles and do not apply to the new keyed pair. Legacy print observations do not establish full fit, opacity or optical commissioning. See the [historical v1.0.1 archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23485390) for the earlier package.
